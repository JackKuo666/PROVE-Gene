# PROVE-Gene

## Evidence-to-Knowledge and File-to-Insight Workflows

PROVE-Gene is an evidence-grounded workbench for gene knowledge discovery and inspectable data analysis. It brings together literature, figures, captions, gene records, and user-uploaded research files in a human-supervised research loop.

## Capabilities

1. **Gene search and literature dialogue** with evidence-aware structured answers.
2. **Literature review assistance** that organizes retrieved evidence into a traceable synthesis.
3. **Multimodal knowledge extraction** from figures and full-text PDFs, including source-linked fields and gene relations.
4. **Evidence-aware graph exploration** based on normalized entities and provenance-linked candidate relations.
5. **File-to-insight analysis**, including inspectable analytical plans, generated tables, visualizations, code, reports, and downloadable outputs.

The current demonstration includes a representative GWAS-to-annotation workflow. All generated results are candidate findings intended for expert review, not biological validation.
